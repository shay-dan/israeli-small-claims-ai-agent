## Rubik font

The project bundles the Rubik typeface from the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rubik) so Hebrew PDFs render consistently across operating systems.

Rubik is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The complete license text is stored in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
